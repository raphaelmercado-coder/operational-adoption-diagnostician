# Public Case: MHS GENESIS Dentrix

This is a real, publicly verifiable case assembled from an official U.S. Government Accountability Office performance audit.

## Diagnostic question

Why did the Dentrix dental module fail to become reliably operational even after DOD completed deployment of the broader MHS GENESIS electronic health record?

## Primary source

U.S. Government Accountability Office, *Electronic Health Records: DOD Has Deployed New System but Challenges Remain*, GAO-24-106187, April 18, 2024.

- Report page: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-106187
- Full report: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-24-106187.pdf
- Accessible report: https://www.gao.gov/assets/870/868847.pdf

GAO conducted the performance audit from August 2022 through April 2024 under generally accepted government auditing standards. Its work included implementation records, risk and issue documentation, program-management meetings, interviews, surveys, and a site visit.
