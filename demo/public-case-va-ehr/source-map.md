# VA EHR Evidence-Packet Source Map

This document makes the blind-test construction auditable. The test began with one real public source: VA OIG Report 20-01930-183. Six evidence artifacts were reconstructed from factual material reproduced or described in that report.

The artifacts are not represented as independently obtained raw memoranda, survey exports, training files, or system records. They are page-sourced reconstructions designed to preserve the evidence while withholding the auditors' diagnosis.

Page numbers below refer to the report's printed page numbers, not the PDF viewer's page count.

## Material deliberately withheld from the blind-test packet

The following parts of the full audit were not carried into the packet as findings or conclusions:

- the executive summary's language naming significant deficiencies in training content, delivery, workflow coverage, and evaluation, printed pages ii-vi;
- the report's conclusion that classroom training and supplemental materials were insufficient, printed pages 43-45;
- the conclusion that the training domain and user-role process left staff unprepared for production work, printed pages 43-44;
- the conclusion that VA OEHRM decision-making failed to address VHA concerns, printed page 44;
- Recommendations 1-11, printed page 46; and
- any sentence whose function was to tell the diagnostician what the primary cause should be.

Facts that also appear inside a withheld section were retained only when the same fact was established elsewhere in the report and could be cited to that evidentiary section.

## Transformation rules

1. Preserve factual chronology, named responsibilities, quantitative results, attributed accounts, and explicit limitations.
2. Paraphrase for compactness without strengthening the source claim.
3. Mark testimony and staff accounts as attributed rather than established fact.
4. Preserve uncertainty, including the 45 percent survey response rate, COVID-19 confounding, and missing technical-operability evidence.
5. Remove OIG findings, conclusions, causal labels, recommendations, and remedial language.
6. Add no case fact from outside VA OIG Report 20-01930-183.
