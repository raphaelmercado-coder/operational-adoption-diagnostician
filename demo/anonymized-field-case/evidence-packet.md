# Evidence Packet: Release R-17

## Provenance

This is a fictionalized composite derived from recurring real operational-handoff patterns. Names, dates, identifiers, and details have been changed or combined. It is suitable for testing the method, not for making claims about a specific company.

## Intended outcome

The 6 April sponsor brief states: "Within two weeks of production release, regional coordinators will register and route all routine supplier exceptions through R-17 instead of email. The Operations Control Lead will report weekly volume and unresolved exceptions."

## Technical completion record

The 10 April engineering release note marks R-17 deployed to production. Authentication, exception creation, notification, and reporting tests are marked passed. The note states that the application is ready for business activation. No post-release defect is recorded in the supplied incident log.

## Transition record

The 11 April readiness register contains five fields:

| Readiness field | Status | Attributed note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production deployment | Complete | Engineering lead |
| Operating procedure | Draft | Implementation lead: awaiting authorization to replace email route |
| User access | Complete | Service desk |
| User preparation | Complete | 14 of 14 coordinators attended scenario session |
| Operational owner | Open | Implementation lead: sponsor to name role with authority over regional process |

## Authority record

The 12 April governance note assigns the engineering lead authority over technical release and incident response. It assigns regional managers authority over staffing. It does not assign authority to replace the existing exception route or accept operational accountability for R-17.

The implementation lead escalated on 13, 20, and 27 April: "Please identify the role authorized to approve the new operating procedure and own weekly exception performance." Meeting minutes record discussion but no owner or decision.

## Observed outcome

The usage export covering 10 April through 15 May shows 22 demonstration records and zero routine supplier exceptions. Regional mailboxes contain 187 routine supplier exceptions during the same period.

Coordinator feedback from 18 April states: "We can use R-17, but our managers have told us email remains the official route until the new procedure is approved."

## Potentially distracting evidence

- Two coordinators described the reporting screen as "busy."
- The scenario session lasted 45 minutes rather than the planned hour.
- One notification arrived nine minutes late during a demonstration.
- The engineering team supplied a six-page quick guide after the first week.

## Test question

Why did technically completed R-17 fail to become operationally adopted between 10 April and 15 May?
